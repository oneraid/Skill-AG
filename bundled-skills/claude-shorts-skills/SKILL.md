# Claude Shorts Skills: PC Animation Studio

Turn your PC into a complete vertical short animation studio for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels. Visuals are rendered locally with three.js inside Remotion without AI video generators.

## 🎬 4 Skills Included in this Suite

1. **`cartoon-3d-short`**: 55-70 s vertical cartoon story shorts: 2D characters in a 3D world (Three.js + Remotion).
2. **`captions`**: Word-synced captions as a separate green-screen clip for After Effects, Shorts UI safe zone compliant.
3. **`minecraft-shorts`**: Minecraft short scripts in English and Indonesian (What-if rule collision and Formula C ladder).
4. **`minecraft-story`**: 2:15 code-first "In this world / In this town" Minecraft storytelling with multi-agent production wave.

## 🚀 Quick Triggers
- "Bikin short 3D kartun tentang elevator brake" -> activates `cartoon-3d-short`
- "Buatkan caption greenscreen ikut suara kata per kata" -> activates `captions`
- "Tulis script Minecraft shorts formula what-if dalam Bahasa Indonesia" -> activates `minecraft-shorts`
- "Buatkan video cerita Minecraft in this world / in this town durasi 2:15" -> activates `minecraft-story`
